V19 changes
- Main-world vegetation is now hard-gated to WORLD_MAIN, preventing grass/crystal decor from leaking into ocean and winter worlds after portal transitions.
- Removed the explicit winter ice-crystal props.
- Player hitscan weapons can intercept incoming submarine heavy rockets; destroyed rockets create a small harmless explosion and HUD message.
- Tank models enlarged by 1.65x and tank hitboxes increased to match the new silhouette.
- Hitscan enemy selection now ignores enemies belonging to another world.
