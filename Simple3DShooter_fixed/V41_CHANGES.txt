V41 CHANGELOG

- Player ammunition is fully replenished on death/respawn.
- Ocean submarines now have 360 HP; close rocket hits apply at least 90% damage, so three rocket hits are sufficient.
- Added a large volumetric 3D submarine destruction effect with expanding debris/shockwave chunks.
- Added a separate world-space ocean sky with a fixed world direction, gradient atmosphere and sun; it is not a camera-locked overlay.
- Buried the complete tunnel network 13 units below the local terrain. Surface gates remain visible; E teleports into/out of the real underground network.
- Removed the old surface-to-underground ramp behaviour.
- Tunnel movement collision now uses the union of the actual tunnel segment contours, including bends/junctions.
- Tunnel projectile/line-of-sight checks now block rays that leave the corridor or cross the floor/arched ceiling.
- Tunnel enemies and drones are constrained by the same corridor contour and cannot walk through tunnel walls.
- Ocean player rockets no longer use the old low ceiling limit; high jetpack shots can travel to their target.
- Source-level checks performed: balanced braces, duplicate declaration check, asset presence and archive structure.
