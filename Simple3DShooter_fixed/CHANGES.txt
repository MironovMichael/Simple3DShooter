Version v6 - settlements and enterable houses
- Added 5 settlements across the map, each with 3 large houses.
- Houses have collision walls and a wide doorway opening, so the player can enter and use them as cover.
- House roofs block line of sight from enemies above/around the building while leaving the interior walkable.
- Houses are integrated into the existing world-object collision and rendering system.
- Preserved previous features: 320 enemies, turning enemies, tanks, drones across tunnel segments, open map edges, and surface traversal over buried tunnels.
