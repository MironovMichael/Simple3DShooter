V43 changes

- ESC pause is now edge-triggered and cannot immediately close the pause menu on the same key press.
- ESC in pause resumes the exact current game state without resetGame().
- Music volume control added to both start menu and pause menu (5% steps, Left/Right).
- Music volume is persisted in settings.cfg and controlled through the Windows MCI waveaudio device.
- System clock moved to the top-left HUD corner.
- Main-world sun is a camera-facing billboard at a world-fixed, phase-driven position, so it visibly moves across the sky during the day.
- Added a smooth raised terrain foundation beneath the two imported WTC complexes to prevent walking under their bases.
