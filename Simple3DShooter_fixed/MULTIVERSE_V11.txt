Multiverse v11
==============
- Ocean and Winter worlds now have distinct enemy variants and movement gimmicks.
- Ocean: fast amphibious raiders with weaving movement and separate composition.
- Winter: frost stalkers with burst movement and heavier composition.
- Ocean-only objects: energy beacons, shipwrecks, icebergs.
- Winter-only objects: pines, radar stations, ice crystals.
- Objects use per-world local coordinates, so they cannot overlap main-world city/settlements/tunnels.
- Their collision radii are separate from ordinary main-world object hitboxes.
- Minimap logic remains world-local; main-world portals stay in the main world.
