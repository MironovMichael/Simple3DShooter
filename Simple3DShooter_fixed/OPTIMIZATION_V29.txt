V29 PERFORMANCE OPTIMIZATION

- Reduced terrain render distance to 260 units and terrain grid from 176/80 to 64 cells.
- Terrain is rendered in one GL_QUADS batch instead of one draw call per terrain cell.
- Reduced object render distance to 210 units.
- Reduced enemy render distance to 200 units with simplified distant enemy LOD.
- Reduced decorative vegetation density and extra clumps.
- Reduced sky dome tessellation to 64x20 and stars to 350.
- Reduced winter snow particles to 220.
- Reduced grass cluster geometry to four blades and batched its quads.
- Reduced fog quality from GL_NICEST to GL_DONT_CARE.
- Default window resolution is capped at 1280x720 and is windowed to avoid rendering a 4K fullscreen surface.
- Reduced sky texture to 2048x1024 and grass texture to 256x256 to lower VRAM use.

The goal is a stable low-end performance profile while retaining the main gameplay systems.
