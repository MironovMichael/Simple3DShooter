Multiverse v12
==============
- Ocean and Winter worlds use separate local coordinate spaces.
- Their objects and enemies are tagged by world and rendered only in that world.
- Added distinct ocean/winter enemy silhouettes and colors.
- Ocean enemies have fin/floatation gear; winter enemies have ice armor.
- Ocean-only objects: beacons, shipwrecks, icebergs.
- Winter-only objects: pines, radar stations, ice crystals.
- Minimap now shows destination portals and unique-world objects in the current world.
- Main-world portal positions were moved to open sectors to avoid city/settlement overlap.
