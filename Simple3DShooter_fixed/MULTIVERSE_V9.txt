Multiverse v9
=============
- Crossing any main-map edge wraps the player to the opposite edge.
- Added Ocean World and Winter World with distinct procedural terrain/materials.
- Main world has six distributed inter-world portals: four to ocean/winter and two to the city.
- Press E near a portal to travel. Each destination world has a return portal at (0,0).
- Portal visuals are separate from collision objects, so they do not create hitbox conflicts.
- Enemies are tagged by world; main-world enemies do not appear in destination worlds.
- Ocean and winter worlds seed their own hostile populations on first visit.
- Existing city, settlements, tunnels and tunnel drones remain in the main world.
