V27 rework

- Removed the previous Earth sky textures and old reference image.
- Added the new user-provided sky reference as a processed seamless equirectangular texture.
- Sky is rendered after the camera matrix is established, eliminating the old dark patch and edge artifacts.
- Sun is positioned in fixed world space and follows the day/night orbit instead of the player.
- Stars are generated from fixed world-space coordinates and slowly rotate as a celestial sphere.
- Replaced the low-resolution grass texture with a 512x512 RGBA atlas of fine blades.
- Grass cards are narrower, more numerous, alpha blended and varied in height/lean.
