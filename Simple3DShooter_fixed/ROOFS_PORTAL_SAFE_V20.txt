V20 FIXES

1. HOUSE ROOFS
- Fixed the world-object terrain snapping that was resetting OBJECT_HOUSE_ROOF Y to ground level.
- Roofs now keep their intended elevation above the 5.5-unit house walls.
- Reworked roof rendering into two visible sloped roof slabs plus a raised ridge.
- Roof dimensions remain conservative enough for collision and portal safety.

2. SAFE RETURN TELEPORTS
- Added a final destination validation before every inter-world teleport.
- Return portals from Ocean/Winter no longer blindly send the player to (0,0).
- The destination is checked against buildings, roofs, trees, skyscrapers, crates, world-specific landmarks and tunnel zones.
- If occupied, the game searches expanding rings for the nearest clear destination.
- The same safety check also protects future portal destinations from newly added map objects.
