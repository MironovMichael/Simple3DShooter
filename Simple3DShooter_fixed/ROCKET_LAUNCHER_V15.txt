Rocket Launcher / Ocean Combat v15
===================================
- Key 4: ROCKET LAUNCHER (Ocean World only).
- Player rockets: 140 direct damage, 18m explosion radius.
- 24 ocean-only rocket ammo crates, +3 reserve rockets each.
- Giant flying submarines: 1600 HP, visible health bars.
- Player rocket explosions damage submarines and nearby enemies.
- Submarine missiles explode and damage the player by blast falloff.
- Rocket ammo crates appear on the ocean minimap.
- Leaving the ocean automatically returns to the pistol.
